# Panasonic RW2 structure and what original DC-S5 files record

## Summary and question addressed

What does a Panasonic RW2 file contain? Which of its properties does Panasonic document, and which are known only from reverse engineering or from a few original DC-S5 files? Panasonic's S5 manual says RAW pictures use the `.RW2` extension and are always recorded at the large 3:2 size. It publishes no format specification and does not state the S5's still-RAW bit depth or compression. [V] The container details come from ExifTool and LibRaw, which are reverse-engineered, and from three owner-provided S5 samples. [I] In those samples an RW2 is a single-IFD, TIFF-like file. Its header has 0x0055 where TIFF has 42, followed by a 16-byte signature. It holds one 14-bit CFA plane in a fixed-size packed encoding (`RawFormat` 6), per-channel black levels, linearity limits, a 1920×1280 JPEG preview and a vendor-private distortion block. [V] for the samples. A fixed-size encoding like this cannot be lossless for every input. [I] This is a research draft, not a decoder specification, and it does not cover S5 modes absent from the samples.

## Evidence and scope

Evidence comes in three kinds, and the labels keep them apart:

1. **Manufacturer documents** (SRC-PB007-01–04). Graded B: vendor manuals and support pages, not a format specification.
2. **Reverse-engineered implementations** (SRC-PB007-06 ExifTool, SRC-PB007-07 LibRaw, SRC-PB007-08 libopenraw). These record what a decoder does. A format fact drawn from them is [I] at grade C, or D for an unexplained secondary statement. Panasonic has not confirmed any of it.
3. **Owner-provided Lumix S5 sample files, not redistributed** (SRC-PB007-09). Three files, inspected read-only on 2026-10-21 with a purpose-written Python 3.14.7 header and IFD reader. ExifTool was not installed on the inspection machine, and nothing was installed. Only format fields were read. A sample observation is [V]/C for those files only. It does not establish a general RW2 or S5 fact.

Standards used for contrast: TIFF 6.0 (SRC-PB007-05) and DNG 1.7.1.0 (SRC-PB003-01).

Out of scope or deferred: pixel decoding, colour matrices and colour rendering, S5 noise and dynamic range (blueprint 08.03), High Resolution mode RW2, burst and 6K/4K photo RAW, other Panasonic bodies (the S5II is a different camera), RWL and older `.RAW` files, and any parser or application recommendation. The firmware-specific S5 capability record belongs to blueprint chapter 08 (`08.02`). This module is the format-level "native RW2 metadata audit" listed for chapter 11.

## Definitions, units and prerequisites

- **IFD**: TIFF image file directory, a counted list of 12-byte entries (tag, type, count, value or offset). TIFF 6.0 §2.
- **CFA**: colour filter array. The 2×2 repeat is written row by row, so RGGB means the top row is red, green and the bottom row is green, blue.
- **DN**: digital number, the stored integer sample value. Black levels and linearity limits are in DN at the stated bit depth.
- **Sensor area and crop**: `SensorWidth`×`SensorHeight` is the stored raster. The `Sensor*Border` and `Crop*` tags give a rectangle inside it.
- **RawFormat**: Panasonic tag 0x002d (`pana_encoding` in LibRaw). Its value meanings are reverse-engineered.
- Prerequisite: [MPKB-11-001](MPKB-11-001.md) for the per-resource taxonomy (container, resource role, sample layout, encoding, demosaic state).

## Mathematical foundations and derivations

**Header.** In TIFF 6.0, bytes 0–1 give the byte order ("II" or "MM"), bytes 2–3 hold 42, and bytes 4–7 hold the offset of the first IFD (SRC-PB007-05 §2, p. 13). In all three samples the first eight bytes are `49 49 55 00 18 00 00 00`: little-endian, identifier 0x0055, IFD0 at offset 24. Bytes 8–23 equal the 16-byte value that ExifTool checks for (`88 e7 74 d8 f8 25 1d 4d 94 7a 6e 77 82 2b 5d 6a`). [V]/C. A strict TIFF 6.0 reader that requires 42 will reject the file. [I]

**Row packing (RawFormat 6, 14-bit).** In every sample, `StripByteCounts` = 35,469,312 bytes and `SensorHeight` = 4016 rows. Each row is therefore 8832 bytes, or 552 blocks of 16 bytes. LibRaw's `panasonicC6_load_raw` decodes 11 samples per 16-byte block at 14 bits and 14 per block at 12 bits. 552 × 11 = 6072 = `SensorWidth`. The arithmetic on these samples is [V]/C. The block interpretation comes from LibRaw and is [I]/C.

**Why it cannot be lossless for every input.** A 16-byte block has 2^128 possible states, and eleven independent 14-bit samples have 2^154 possible combinations. A fixed-size block cannot give every combination its own encoding, so some distinct inputs must share one. That follows from the fixed block size alone, and the byte count is the same in all three samples. On average each sample gets 128/11 ≈ 11.64 stored bits. [I]/C. How large the loss is, and where it falls, is [U]. LibRaw's decoder reads a 2-bit selector for each group of three samples and multiplies by 1, 2, 4 or 16. That points to a quantization step that depends on brightness, but it is inferred from decoder code. Panasonic has not said so. [I]/C

## Reference algorithm and pseudocode

An outline for collecting evidence, consistent with MPKB-11-001. It is not a decoder.

1. Read 8 bytes. Require "II" or "MM". Record the 16-bit identifier as observed (0x2A for TIFF, 0x55 for Panasonic). Do not treat 0x55 as TIFF conformance.
2. If the identifier is 0x55 and the IFD0 offset is at least 0x18, compare bytes 8–23 with ExifTool's 16-byte signature and record whether they match. ExifTool classifies a mismatch as "RAW", meaning an older Panasonic file.
3. Parse IFD0, bounds-checking the entry count, value offsets and file size. Tags below 0x0100 are Panasonic-private in this IFD, so do not give them the EXIF or TIFF meanings of the same numbers. LibRaw switches to Panasonic interpretation when tag 0x0001 has count 4.
4. Record `BitsPerSample`, `Compression`, `RawFormat`, `SamplesPerPixel`, `CFAPattern`, sensor size, borders and crop. Check that `StripByteCounts` equals rows × (width / samples per block) × 16 for the claimed encoding. If it does not, mark the encoding unknown and do not guess.
5. Record black levels and linearity limits as observed values with their bit depth. Label any decoder adjustment (+15, −64) separately as a decoder convention.
6. Treat `JpgFromRaw` as its own rendered-preview resource (MPKB-11-001: the preview is not the primary image). Check its SOI and SOF markers and record its dimensions.
7. Keep `DistortionInfo` and other vendor blocks as opaque bytes, plus any reverse-engineered interpretation labelled with its source and version. Never apply them as verified corrections.

## Alternative methods and comparative evidence

**DNG as a contrast** (SRC-PB003-01, DNG 1.7.1.0). DNG keeps the TIFF magic number 42 and identifies itself with `DNGVersion`. Black and white levels are public tags (`BlackLevel` 50714, p. 28; `WhiteLevel` 50717, p. 29). Lens corrections are published opcodes (`OpcodeList3`, p. 58; `WarpRectilinear`, p. 106; `FixVignetteRadial`, p. 110). RW2 has fields that play similar roles, such as per-channel black levels, linearity limits and `DistortionInfo`, but their meanings are known only through reverse engineering. The DNG definitions are [V]/A and the comparison is [I]/C. Converting RW2 to DNG puts the values into documented tags, but the converter itself has to read RW2 by reverse engineering. That adds a layer of interpretation and is not an independent check. [I]

**ExifTool and LibRaw.** The two use the same tag numbers but do different jobs. ExifTool names and prints metadata. LibRaw changes values for decoding (black offsets, maximum, CFA phase). Where they disagree, record both readings. Do not pick one by preference. [D]

**Secondary descriptions.** libopenraw's RW2 page (SRC-PB007-08) gives the header as `IIU\x00\x08\x00\x00\x00` and says the CFA layout "is BGGR." The S5 samples contradict both: IFD0 is at 0x18, and CFAPattern is 1, which is RGGB in ExifTool's mapping. The page names no camera model and has no date. Treat it as D-grade background that is wrong for these samples, not as a general fact.

## Edge cases, failure modes and safeguards

- **Mode coverage.** The S5 manual describes burst and High Resolution modes; High Resolution RAW is 12000×8000 (p. 221). Panasonic documents 14-bit RAW, falling to 12-bit in burst, only for the S5II (SRC-PB007-04). Do not assume either applies to the original S5. In LibRaw, RawFormat 6 can be either 11 samples per block (14-bit) or 14 per block (12-bit), and the data size decides which. [I]/C. No 12-bit or High Resolution S5 file was inspected.
- **Unknown values.** If RawFormat, Compression or BitsPerSample is outside known decoder support, the result must say unsupported. If the strip size does not match the declared layout, the result must say unknown. [D]
- **Preview confusion.** The 1920×1280 JPEG is already rendered, has its own EXIF, and is much smaller than the 6000×4000 crop. It is not the primary image (MPKB-11-001).
- **Applied versus recorded corrections.** The manual's [Vignetting Comp.] setting defaults to ON for photos (pp. 354, 531) and applies to recorded pictures. Whether it changes RW2 pixel data, or is stored as a parameter, is [U]. ExifTool's PanasonicRaw table has no vignetting tag. `DistortionInfo` looks like parameters for a later correction, which would mean the raw data were not warped, but that reading is [I]/D and unverified.
- **Malformed input.** The 0x55 identifier and the signature do not prove the file is complete. Bound every offset. LibRaw rejects Panasonic files whose data size does not match by setting `raw_count = 0`.
- **Private fields.** Panasonic MakerNotes and EXIF also carry personal or identifying data such as serial numbers, timestamps and owner fields. An evidence extractor must read only an allowlist of format fields.

## Panasonic LUMIX-specific notes

- [V]/B Panasonic DC-S5 Operating Instructions DVQP2213ZD: RAW pictures have the `RW2` extension (p. 479). RAW is always recorded at [L] size and 3:2 (p. 87). High Resolution mode can save RAW, and that RAW is always 12000×8000 (pp. 221–222). The firmware 2.0 supplement adds 12-bit RAW video output over HDMI (p. F-13). That output is not an RW2 file.
- [U] Neither this manual edition nor Panasonic's spec page (SRC-PB007-02) gives the bit depth, compression or container of original-S5 still RAW. The 14-bit figure here was observed in three samples. Panasonic does not document it.
- [V]/B Panasonic's spec page gives a full-frame CMOS sensor with a primary colour filter, 24.2 MP effective and 25.28 MP total, and an [L] 3:2 size of 6000×4000. The samples' stored raster of 6072×4016 (24.39 MP) falls between the effective and total pixel counts. That comparison is [I].
- LibRaw at the pinned commit lists "Panasonic DC-S5" as supported and has a DC-S5 colour-matrix entry (`colordata.cpp` line 1292). That is LibRaw's support claim, not a validation. The matrix values are out of scope here.

## Video/cinema extension

Does not apply to RW2. S5 RAW video leaves the camera over HDMI and is recorded on an external recorder as Blackmagic RAW or ProRes RAW (SRC-PB007-01 p. F-13; SRC-PB007-03 Ver. 2.0 and 2.3). It follows the recorder's format and the ProRes RAW notes in MPKB-11-001, not RW2.

## Implementation notes for software

This module recommends no app behaviour. A future parser should keep three layers apart: the bytes and values it observed, reverse-engineered interpretations (tagged with tool, version and commit), and facts Panasonic documents. Any decoder choice belongs in the Perception Editor repository and needs its own licence review. LibRaw is dual-licensed under LGPL-2.1 and CDDL-1.0. ExifTool is distributed under the same terms as Perl. Neither licence was reviewed for adoption here.

## Open questions and recommended experiments

- Does any original-S5 mode write 12-bit RW2, for example burst, 6K/4K photo extraction or another mode? Needs authorized samples from each mode.
- What does a High Resolution mode RW2 contain: dimensions, RawFormat, Multishot value, CFA layout?
- How much does RawFormat 6 lose? Needs flat-field or ramp captures and an independent decode, with the owner's authorization. That is a measurement, not a metadata read.
- Is the CFA phase at the crop origin really RGGB? Needs a decoded capture of a colour target.
- Does [Vignetting Comp.] change RW2 pixel data, or only the JPEG? Needs paired ON/OFF captures of a flat field.
- What do `PanasonicRawVersion` 0400, the Gamma tag, tags 0x0033–0x0038 and tags 0x011a–0x0126 mean?
- Do files from firmware Ver. 2.7–2.9 differ?
