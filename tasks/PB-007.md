## Gate results (second commit)

- **Gate 1, ExifTool 13.55 (Homebrew bottle `13.55_1`):** agrees with the Python reader on every compared field (CFA, bit depth, Compression and RawFormat, black and linearity levels, sensor size, borders and crop, strip size, preview size and byte length, distortion flag, firmware bytes, diffraction flag, Gamma). No disagreements. ExifTool also decodes the distortion parameters, which differ between files. CLM-PB007-21.
- **Gate 2a, CFA:** RGGB at the crop origin in all three samples, from green-pair statistics, preview chroma correlation and as-shot WB on neutral blocks. [V]/C; [I]/C for all S5 files. CLM-PB007-22.
- **Gate 2b, RawFormat 6:** a numpy re-implementation matches rawpy/LibRaw 0.22.1 bit-for-bit. 7.5–11.7% of visible pixels use step 2, 4 or 16 DN; 1.9–3.4% use step 16. [V]/C. The actual error is [U] without a lossless reference. A histogram cannot show it. CLM-PB007-23–25.
- **Gate 2c, vignetting:** MakerNote 0x008a (`ShadingCompensation`) is Off in all three files, so no ON/OFF comparison is possible. Recorded as needing a new pair of shots. CLM-PB007-26.
- Also observed: BurstMode On in two samples that are still 14-bit (CLM-PB007-27). LibRaw's visible area differs from Panasonic's crop (CLM-PB007-28).
